Mobile network–

The A7670 modem connects to the mobile network the way a phone does: LTE (4G) Cat-1 for data, falling back to GSM (2G). It talks to one cell tower at a time, the serving cell. With a SIM it can send positions where there is no Wi-Fi. The tracker uploads over Wi-Fi when it is connected and switches to mobile data when it is out of range.

GPS–

Each navigation satellite broadcasts its exact position and the time from an atomic clock. The receiver measures how long each signal took to arrive (≈ 67 ms from 20 000 km). That delay times the speed of light is the distance. With distances to four or more satellites it can solve for latitude, longitude, altitude and the error of its own cheap clock.

The L76K tracks GPS (USA) together with GLONASS (Russia) or BeiDou (China), whichever the module is set to. More satellites spread across the sky give a more precise fix.
